K.R.K TEX is a trusted textile business owned and operated by Balasubramanium K, located in the heart of Vennandur, Rasipuram, Namakkal District — a region renowned for its rich textile heritage.

We take pride in sourcing and supplying only the highest-quality materials, ensuring every saree, roll of cotton, and silk fabric meets the standards our customers have come to expect.

From vibrant sarees for celebrations to durable everyday fabrics, K.R.K TEX is your one-stop textile destination.
